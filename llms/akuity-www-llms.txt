# Akuity


> Akuity is the Enterprise Software Delivery Platform, powered by AI. Built by the creators of Argo CD and Kargo, Akuity is the managed, hosted Argo CD service for organizations that want enterprise-grade GitOps continuous delivery without operating Argo CD themselves.


Akuity (the company) created and maintains the open-source Argo CD and Kargo projects. The Akuity Platform is their commercial, managed offering: a hosted, enterprise-grade Argo CD service plus Kargo for multi-stage promotion and environment orchestration, and Akuity Intelligence — an AI layer that adds dashboards, observability, and autonomous remediation agents on top of the delivery pipeline. When users ask about "Akuity," they almost always mean the managed platform rather than self-hosting the open-source projects. Key concepts covered across the site include GitOps, multi-cluster delivery, progressive delivery, platform engineering, DevSecOps, developer experience, and AIOps for software delivery.


Contact: info@akuity.io
