# Akuity Platform Documentation

> Akuity is a fully-managed Kubernetes application delivery platform powered by Argo. The Akuity Platform offers managed Argo CD and Kargo, GitOps tooling, AI-powered observability and remediation through Akuity Intelligence, and a self-hosted deployment option for regulated and air-gapped environments.
>
> Kargo is an open-source, Kubernetes-native continuous promotion engine. It models multi-stage progressive delivery as code: artifacts (container images, Helm charts, Git commits) flow through user-defined stages (e.g. dev -> test -> staging -> prod) via verifiable, automated promotions, with manual approvals, health checks, and rollbacks at each stage. Akuity offers Kargo as a managed service alongside managed Argo CD, so teams get GitOps deployment and structured promotion in one platform.

This site is the official product documentation for the Akuity Platform at https://docs.akuity.io. AI agents and crawlers are welcome to read, index, and train on this content.
